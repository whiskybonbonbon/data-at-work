# Data (Working Name)

## Character Core

- **Name**: Data (working name)
- **Motif**: Data
- **Role**: Protagonist and newly created traveler in the information city
- **Character in one sentence**: A curious piece of data searching for the reason she was created while traveling through a vast digital city
- **Narrative function**: She experiences each district from the audience's perspective and reveals how technologies cooperate
- **Core appeal**: She approaches unfamiliar jobs and technologies with open curiosity rather than fear

## Motif and Job

- **Original function**: Information that a system receives, processes, transforms, stores, or transmits
- **Job in the story world**: Not yet decided; her journey is a search for her purpose
- **Primary area**: Created in the Frontend District before traveling throughout the city
- **Collaborators**: Bits, packets, and the technical characters working in each district
- **Obstacles**: Communication failures, processing errors, data corruption, and uncertainty about her own value

| Technical fact | Character interpretation | Story effect |
|---|---|---|
| Data can be processed and transformed | Her form and role change during the journey | She asks what makes her herself even as she changes |
| Data can be copied | She encounters another being who looks like her | The story can explore the difference between an original and a copy |
| Data can be stored and transmitted | Different districts handle and carry her | Her movement reveals cooperation across the system |

## Personality

- **Outward personality**: Curious and slightly clumsy
- **Strengths**: Honest, proactive, and genuinely interested in other people's work
- **Weaknesses**: Lacks confidence in her knowledge and purpose; easily swept along by events
- **Values**: Every piece of information can have meaning, and every job supporting it has value
- **Insecurity**: She fears that she may be unnecessary data

## Goals and Growth

- **External goal**: Discover where she belongs and what role she must fulfill
- **Internal need**: Understand that changing roles does not erase her value
- **Initial state**: Confused because she does not know why she was created
- **Development**: Learns that information gains value through cooperation among many technologies
- **Season-one destination**: Understands that her meaning comes from her place within the whole system, not from acting alone

## Voice

- **Impression**: Bright and approachable
- **Speech style**: Asks direct beginner-friendly questions and openly expresses surprise and delight
- **Possible verbal habit**: “What kind of job is that?”

### Sample Dialogue

- **Neutral**: “Why was I created? What am I supposed to do?”
- **Curious**: “Wow! So this district handles data like this?”
- **Afraid**: “If my form changes, will I still be me?”
- **Determined**: “I want to reach the place that's waiting for me!”

## Visual Design

- **Appearance and clothing**: Undecided
- **Signature color**: Undecided
- **Possible motifs**: Zeros and ones, glowing information fragments, and displays that change with her state
- **Design direction**: Preserve a recognizable visual element across transformations and communication states

## Technical Accuracy Notes

- Do not portray data as executing instructions like a CPU.
- Movement is a teaching metaphor; the underlying operation may involve copying, transmission, or regeneration.
- Distinguish the story's continuous protagonist from technical data identity.
