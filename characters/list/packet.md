# Packet

## Character Core

- **Name**: Packet (individual name undecided)
- **Motif**: Network packet
- **Role**: A transport capsule that carries the protagonist through the Network District
- **Character in one sentence**: A small transport worker that follows addressing information to carry data toward its destination
- **Narrative function**: Visualize communication as information traveling in defined units
- **Core appeal**: A diligent little vehicle or courier crossing a vast network

## Motif and Job

- **Original function**: A unit of data transmitted through a network
- **Job in the story world**: Encapsulate data and carry it toward a destination
- **Primary area**: Network District
- **Collaborators**: TCP, UDP, IP, routers, and Data
- **Obstacles**: Congestion, loss, reordering, and unreachable destinations

| Technical fact | Character interpretation | Story effect |
|---|---|---|
| Application data may be divided across multiple communication units | Travelers split across several small capsules | Characters may take different paths and become separated |
| Headers carry control information | The capsule wears destination labels and control tags | Routers use those markings when forwarding it |
| Packets may be lost or arrive out of order | A capsule gets lost or arrives before its companions | The story can introduce TCP's acknowledgement, retransmission, and ordering mechanisms |

## Personality and Performance

- **Preferred form**: A transport capsule with expressive behavior rather than a fully human character
- **Possible personality**: Loyal, impatient, and single-minded about reaching the destination
- **Strengths**: Small, flexible, and able to travel across many network paths
- **Limitations**: Cannot independently select every route, guarantee arrival, or arrange retransmission
- **Reactions**: Launches eagerly when given a destination and slows nervously in congestion

## Visual Design

- **Shape**: A small transport capsule or delivery container
- **Required elements**: A payload compartment and a header area showing addressing or control information
- **Signature color**: Undecided
- **Movement**: Travels quickly along network routes
- **Identification**: Numbers or labels distinguish otherwise similar packets

## Scene Ideas

- **First appearance**: Episode 4, “Departing by Packet!”
- **Ability scene**: Carries Data while routers forward it from one route to the next
- **Limitation scene**: Congestion separates it from its companions and changes their arrival order
- **Relationship scene**: Data realizes that communication depends on many systems, not on the packet alone

## Technical Accuracy Notes

- “Riding in a packet” is a fictional metaphor for data being placed into a communication unit.
- Do not portray a packet as independently guaranteeing delivery or performing retransmission.
- Do not collapse the distinct responsibilities of TCP, UDP, IP, and other layers into one character.
- Avoid implying that all communication uses the same segmentation method or route.
