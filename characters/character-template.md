## 14. Consistency Checklist

- [ ] The motif or role is visible in the character's appearance
- [ ] The personality connects to the character's job or abilities
- [ ] The character has both strengths and weaknesses
- [ ] The goal and reason for acting are clear
- [ ] The character's role does not overlap too heavily with another character
- [ ] The character can be identified from their speech alone
- [ ] Their abilities have limitations that prevent easy solutions
- [ ] Established details are used in actual scenes
- [ ] The character changes or proves a belief over the course of the story
- [ ] The boundary between fact and fiction can be explained
