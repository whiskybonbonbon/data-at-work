# Data at Work

> Even a single piece of data is supported by countless jobs.

Data at Work is a creative project set inside computers and web services. It presents technical systems as characters whose jobs and adventures reveal how technology works.

The story follows Data-chan, a newly created piece of data, as she travels through the frontend, network, backend, and database districts. Along the way, she discovers why she was created and meets the many workers who support information systems. The project aims to give beginners an approachable introduction to technology while offering experienced engineers recognizable details and discoveries.

## Creative Principles

1. Preserve the essential roles and relationships of the technologies being portrayed.
2. Clearly distinguish fictional metaphors from technical reality.
3. Give each episode one central learning theme.
4. Keep the surface story accessible to beginners while including details experienced engineers can appreciate.
5. Maintain a consistent identity for the protagonist even when the story involves copying, transforming, or deleting data.

## Creating Characters

1. Copy `characters/character-template.md` when creating a character profile.
2. Document the technical facts behind the motif separately from the fictional metaphor.
3. Define the character's limitations and potential sources of misunderstanding as well as their role and strengths.
4. Record additions and changes in `characters/character-list.md`.
